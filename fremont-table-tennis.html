( Case study 01 ) — Table tennis · Top project

FREMONT TABLE TENNIS Academy.

Client
Fremont Table Tennis Academy
Location
Fremont & San Ramon, CA
Role
UX & UI redesign, build
Live site
fremonttabletennis.com ↗

The brief

WHICH CLASS IS right for me?

An award-winning academy with two locations, classes for ages 3 to adult, camps, weekly leagues and a pro shop. Every visitor arrives with the same question — so the redesign answers it first.

01

Find a class, fast

Clear paths for kids, adults, camps and private lessons.

02

Camps up front

Camp registration promoted at the top of every page.

03

One tap to act

Call, text and register buttons always within reach.

Before and after

Before After

Drag the handle · before on the left, after on the right

On a phone

Most parents book from the sideline or the car. The redesign was laid out phone-first: one column, the class finder at the top, and call, text and register buttons that stay in reach the whole way down.

Result

The before and after are both on this page.

A parent landing on the old site had to work out where to go. On the new one the classes, the camps and the phone number are the first things on the screen, and the academy finally reads the way it talks about itself.

No sign-up figure is published here. The academy’s numbers are theirs, not mine — so drag the slider above and judge this one on the work.
