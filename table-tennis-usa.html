What I found

HALF OF CHECKOUTS WERE
abandoned.

Only 5.5% of July sessions reached a cart, and about half of the checkouts that started were never finished. Weak product discovery, thin trust signals, unclear variants and shipping costs that appeared too late.

All nine problems found in the audit
  • No conversion-focused homepage layout
  • Weak product discovery for brand-loyal players
  • Thin trust signals on the pages that needed them
  • Unclear variant selection on equipment
  • Shipping cost arriving as a surprise at checkout
  • Broken breadcrumbs and SEO heading structure
  • Product pages inconsistent from one to the next
  • Video content sending visitors away from the store
  • Email address exposed in markup, inviting spam

What changed

01

Homepage

Rebuilt around brand discovery — Butterfly, GEWO, Stiga, Victas.

02

Trust

Trust signals added where shoppers hesitate.

03

Product pages

Reworked so variants are clear.

04

Shipping

Options rebuilt, plus a free-shipping bar at $75.

93% of carts reached checkout, against 52% in July.

Straight from the Shopify dashboard
