( About ) — A designer who picked sport on purpose

HI, I’M
Akintayo.

Web designer & developer

Akingbehin Akintayo

I’m a web designer and developer from Osun State, Nigeria, building websites for sports businesses around the world.

I started in table tennis — stores, clubs and coaches — and learned how players actually choose a blade, a class or a coach. Now I bring that same care to football, cricket, volleyball and hockey.

When you work with me you work with one person: I read your email, go through your site, design the pages and write the code. No account managers, no hand-offs.

Off the clock

I’m a Doctor of Pharmacy candidate at Obafemi Awolowo University. I play chess, collect gadgets and enjoy books, restaurants and travel. Beyond sport, I’ve also built the Shopify store for ORIMI, a fine jewellery house shipping to more than 25 countries.
