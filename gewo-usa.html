The brief

BE THE BRAND AND the shop.

The US store of a table tennis equipment manufacturer. The catalogue runs from shakehand and penhold blades through four categories of rubber, indoor and outdoor tables, balls, apparel, footwear and maintenance items down to edge tape and glue.

Those are not one kind of purchase. Apparel is browsed, rubber is specified, a table is researched, edge tape is re-ordered. The store has to let all four happen without the brand story blocking someone who came to buy glue.
