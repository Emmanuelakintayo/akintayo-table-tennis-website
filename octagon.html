The project

THREE AUDIENCES, one site.

Octagon works in sports and entertainment marketing, so the site has to serve three very different visitors at once: brands looking for a partner, athletes looking for representation, and people looking for a job.

The design keeps those paths apart and obvious from the first screen, and lets the campaign work carry the visual weight rather than the copy. Everything else gets out of the way of the thing a visitor actually came to look at.
