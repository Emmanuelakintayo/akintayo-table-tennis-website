Known distortions

TWO REASONS THE RATE
lies.

August’s traffic was not real

43,520 sessions against July’s 16,349, almost all of it Singapore going from a handful to 21,500. By September it was back to 789. Roughly 25,000 sessions that were never going to buy pushed August down to 0.68%.

Shopify changed how it counts sessions

A notice on the analytics page says sessions and related metrics may shift from 21 September 2026. Any rate spanning that date is not like-for-like. Order counts are unaffected, which is why they are the headline.

Deliberately absent

NO REVENUE. NO CUSTOMER data.

The store’s sales figures exist and I have seen them. They are the client’s to publish, not mine, so no dollar amount appears anywhere on this site. Nor does anything that could identify a customer.

Nothing here is a projection of what your store would do. It is a record of what one store did, with the parts that are not flattering left in.
